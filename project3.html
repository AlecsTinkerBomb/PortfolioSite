Final Work

Civil Culinary School

The Civil Culinary School project proposes an adaptive reuse of the Porter Produce Warehouse in Fayetteville, Arkansas, transforming an existing industrial structure into a civic culinary school — the Porter Center for Food and Beverage Studies. With the project one precedent in mind, the Porter House is expanded through means of structure, sequence, movement and facade composition. With the Rosenthal precedent in mind, a rectangular composition was focused with the intent of keeping the interior ambiguous. The glazing on the facade contrasted that, hinting at the sequence of the building before entry. Tasked with casting models, two models were produced with each scheme, one representing solid and the other void.

Part 1

Urban Vertical Precedent Study

The Rosenthal Center for Contemporary Art by Zaha Hadid served as a foundational precedent throughout the semester, establishing a framework for how architecture engages its urban context. Its "urban carpet" — a ground plane extending from the sidewalk into a suspended ramped mezzanine — became a recurring reference point for decisions around circulation, threshold, and interior-exterior continuity. Returning to Zaha's design intent across the semester reinforced how a single cohesive move can drive an entire building's spatial narrative.
